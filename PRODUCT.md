# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Agent Workflow Roast is for developers and agent-tool builders reviewing their own recent Codex sessions. They use it locally, often inside a repo, when they need a concise read on work habits, prompt quality, friction patterns, and reusable process improvements without exposing private session data.

## Product Purpose

Agent Workflow Roast turns bounded, redacted local agent history into a coaching report with evidence. Success means a user can quickly see what went well, what got in the way, which prompts or habits to improve, and what durable rules or artifacts to carry into future work.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, kind, concrete. The product should feel candid and useful, with enough personality to make the feedback memorable, but never mean, vague, or theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid a punitive scorecard. The interface must not shame the user, over-focus on grades, or turn coaching into gamified judgment. It should also avoid generic SaaS-dashboard tropes, vague productivity claims, and raw log-dump density that buries the point.

## Design Principles

- Lead with evidence, then coaching.
- Make the next action obvious.
- Keep the roast human, not hostile.
- Respect local privacy as part of the product experience.
- Prefer dense clarity over decorative dashboard noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for report UI and playground surfaces. Preserve readable contrast, keyboard-accessible interactions, clear focus states, semantic structure, and reduced-motion alternatives for any animation.
